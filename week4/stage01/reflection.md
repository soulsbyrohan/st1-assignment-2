# Stage 1 Reflection

I built a simple Python prototype that stores appointments in a list, using a dictionary for each appointment and functions to book and display appointments. I added basic validation for empty patient names.

AI helped me understand that my prototype lacked a double-booking check and unique appointment IDs. It also explained how docstrings improve readability.

AI made assumptions: it assumed no validation was needed and did not check for double-booking. I had to identify these gaps myself.

I verified AI output by running the code, testing normal and unusual inputs, and comparing behaviour with requirements.

The engineering work that remained for me included adding validation for practitioner name and time, implementing the double-booking check, and planning for persistence. AI provided suggestions, but I had to judge which were in scope and take responsibility for correctness.
